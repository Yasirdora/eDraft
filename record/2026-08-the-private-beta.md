# The private beta

At the end of August 2026 a private beta began, with the macOS application and
the iPhone and iPad applications in testers' hands.

## What it was for

The applications share one engine and one editing model, and a private beta is
where that claim meets real scripts. Testers worked on documents they already
had, in the formats they already worked in, on more than one device.

## Continuity

Documents are files. On Apple platforms they live in iCloud Drive when a writer
wants them to, which is how a script started on a phone is finished at a desk.
Nothing about that requires an account or an eDraft server in the path, and it
is optional: a document kept on one device never leaves it. iCloud is Apple's,
with the security that carries.

## Open

Revision colours, locked pages and scene numbering as commands were still
missing. They are the production layer.
