# iPad, and the research

## The applications

eDraft 2.1, on 10 November 2026, gave each person's notes a colour of their own
and added labels. eDraft 2.2, on 14 November 2026, brought the application to
iPad, in the same free (preview) App Store download as the iPhone and the Mac,
and gave every scene in the navigator a menu of its own.

On iPad it is the same application as on iPhone, with the same engine and the
same editing model. A version for Apple Vision Pro is in test.

## The research

On 16 November 2026 the design research behind eDraft was published as a
report: *The Writer Never Formats: What belongs in a screenplay editor, and
where it should sit* (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24285828, CC BY 4.0). It asks what
belongs at the centre of a screenplay editor, what should be optional, and what
should stay out, and it gives every feature one of four places and a status.
See [Research](../docs/research.md).
