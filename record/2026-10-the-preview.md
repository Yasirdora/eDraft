# The preview

In October 2026 the macOS application was released to the public on the App
Store as a preview, free, for macOS 26 and later.

## What is in it

The writing surface: element formatting as it is typed, Tab and Return
choreography, character prediction, find and print. The title page as a sheet,
the character thread as the Cast tab, the scene navigator alongside the page.
Interchange in both directions with Final Draft documents and Fountain, and
export to PDF and Word.

Documents are files on the writer's disk. On Apple platforms they can live in
iCloud Drive so that one script opens on more than one device; the choice is
the writer's, and there is no account and no eDraft server either way.

## What is not in it

The production layer: revision colours, locked pages, and scene numbering as
commands. The iPhone and iPad applications, which share the engine and the
editing model with the Mac, were still in beta.

## Why it is a preview

The writing surface and interchange are ready for real scripts. The production
layer is not written, and releasing it as anything other than a preview would
claim something the application cannot yet do.
